Three modelling choices

  • Exposure: every claim happens within 363 days of policy start, so we assume a 12-month term. Exposure = min(1, days since start ÷ 365). Counting policies instead would flatter old business.
  • Earned premium = annual premium × exposure.
  • Incurred proxy: Paid → paid amount; Denied → 0; Open → claim amount × the paid ratio of the same peril.
Result: loss ratio 0.70 overall · Auto 0.53 · Home 1.03.
Assumptions: 12-month term, cancelled policies treated as full exposure, incurred is a proxy not audited accounting. No expenses, reserves or IBNR in the files.
