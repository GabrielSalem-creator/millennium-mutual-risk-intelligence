Pattern 5 · Bundles and renewals

Customers with Auto + Home cost us less. Customers who claim leave more.

In kid words: people who buy two things from us tend to have fewer accidents. And people who just had a claim are much more likely to be gone next year.
1 DETECT1,405 of 3,276 customers hold both lines. Their loss ratio is lower.
→ bundle LR 0.66 vs 0.75
2 HYPOTHESISEBundling attracts careful people (selection), or the discount makes them stay.
→ selection is likely
3 RESEARCHJ.D. Power 2022 US study: about 95% retention for bundlers vs 85% for non-bundlers.
→ US survey, context only
4 TESTCompare claim frequency and price.
→ fewer claims, only 6% cheaper
5 PEERNon-renewal by city and price quartile.
→ flat, so claims drive it
6 VERDICTBundle = better risk. Claims = churn.
→ keep good claimants

Verdict

SUPPORTED BY DATABundled customers are our better risks, so a deeper bundle discount is affordable. The claim-to-exit link is a service question: who is leaving, the good risks or the bad? We can't tell yet because the files hold no reason-for-leaving field.
  • J.D. Power 2022 US Home Insurance Study: US, survey-based. It measures retention, not loss ratio.
  • Research gap: no loss-ratio study on bundled vs unbundled customers found.
