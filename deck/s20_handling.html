Montreal's claims take longer to close. Is that the adjusters?

In kid words: three teams close claims at different speeds, but each team only looks after certain cities. So we can't tell "slow team" from "tricky city" without testing.

→ 3 rival explanations
3 RESEARCHSearched for public benchmarks of Canadian claim cycle times by city. None found.
→ gap stated, nothing invented
4 TESTCompare the same peril across teams.
→ Team C still slower
5 PEERTeams are geographic, so no city is handled by two teams.
→ cannot separate team from city
6 VERDICTReal delay, cause unknown.
→ pilot: swap some files
