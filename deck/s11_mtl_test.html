Water claims by calendar month (Montreal vs Toronto)

Montreal: 36 of 77 claims in Mar–May, plus an October bump. Toronto is flat all year. That is a seasonal signature, consistent with thaw and rain. It is not one storm: only 3 Montreal days had even 2 water claims.
