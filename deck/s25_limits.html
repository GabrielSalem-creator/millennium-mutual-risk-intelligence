What is measured, what is assumed, what we still need.

Measured from the files

  • Counts, claim frequency per exposure-year, severity
  • B07: 60 theft claims vs about 3 expected
  • Montreal Home water 3.0× Toronto (95% CI 1.95–4.63)
  • Cleaning: 15 duplicates, 140 text premiums, 108 DD/MM/YYYY dates

Assumed

  • 12-month policy term (all claims fall within 363 days of start)
  • Cancelled policies earn a full year
  • Incurred proxy: paid; denied = 0; open claims estimated
  • 30% expense load, 5% margin, price sensitivity
  • Currency unspecified

Not in the data

  • Expenses, reserves, IBNR, reinsurance, investment income
  • Postal codes and property details
  • Quote and conversion records
  • Reason a customer left

Next data to request, in order

  1. Policy start and end dates, earned premium by month — turns our exposure assumption into fact.
  2. Insured postal code and property data (age, basement, plumbing, mitigation) — tests why Montreal water is 3× the peer cities.
  3. Expenses, commissions, case reserves, IBNR — turns the loss ratio into a real profit number.
  4. Quote and conversion records — replaces our price-sensitivity guess with measured behaviour.
  5. Referral of broker B07 to the special investigations unit — consistent with early-claim fraud or adverse selection, not proof.
Research gaps: no like-for-like Montreal vs Toronto housing-age source; no monthly Quebec claim-peak source; no evidence that Toronto's flood subsidy works; no loss-ratio study on bundles; no public data on claim cycle times by city.
