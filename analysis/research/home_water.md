# Home water-damage drivers: sourced evidence (Canada; Montreal focus)
Compiled 2026-11-14. Status tags: VERIFIED = number read on the fetched page; SNIPPET = only seen in search-result summary; UNVERIFIED = could not read the source (several PDFs and two Canadian Underwriter pages failed to fetch/parse).
Headline gap: I found NO Canadian public source giving a quantified claim-frequency/severity relativity for building age, plumbing material, plex vs single-family, or backwater valve/sump pump. Only vendor/US figures exist for leak shut-off devices.

## 1. Share of claims by cause
F1. Quebec 2024: water damage = 70.9% of home-insurance claim COSTS (CAD ~3.3B total), fire 20.5%, theft 1.6%, wind 1.1%; ground water + sewer infiltration ~48.4% of cost.
 - Source: IBC Quebec, "Distribution of losses, home insurance 2024" chart, https://infoassurance.ca/media/nqja231i/ibc-quebec-graph-distribution-losses-home-insurance-2024.pdf (2025 publication; 95% of personal-lines market, not extrapolated to 100%).
 - Status: SNIPPET/UNVERIFIED. PDF text could not be extracted by WebFetch or locally; read the PDF directly before use. Limit: cost shares, not counts; Quebec-wide, not Montreal.
F2. Allstate Canada: water damage >40% of all its home claims 2021-2025; external water (storm/overland/sewer backup) = 24% of 2025 claims and ~31% of 2021-25 water claims; external-water claims +94% in 2025 vs 2024.
 - Source: "Allstate Canada sees 94% surge in external water claims in 2025", Insurance Business Canada, 24 Feb 2026, https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/ca/news/claims/allstate-canada-sees-94-surge-in-external-water-claims-in-2025-566364.aspx. VERIFIED (page read). Limit: one insurer's book, no regional split, no age/deductible info.
F3. Quebec: sewer backup + ground infiltration = one-third of water-damage incidents; average claim for this type >$18,000 in 2023.
 - Source: infoassurance.ca (IBC Quebec consumer site), "How to protect yourself from sewer back-ups", https://infoassurance.ca/en/blog/home/how-to-protect-yourself-from-sewer-back-ups (undated page; 2023 figure). VERIFIED. Limit: ">$18,000" is a floor, not a mean; no source methodology.
F4. National: "water damage = leading cause of property loss, more claims than fire and theft combined"; average interior water-damage claim >$17,000; ~1 in 3 Canadians wrongly think standard policy covers flood; nearly half lack sewer-backup/overland cover.
 - Source: sponsored article (Moen Canada) citing IBC, insurance-canada.ca, 28 Jan 2026, https://insurance-canada.ca/?p=84800. VERIFIED that the article says this; IBC primary not located. Limit: sponsored content, IBC citation unlinked.
F5. IBC (via secondary report, mid-2026): external water (overland + sewer backup) nearly a quarter of home claims; 94% of homes can access overland cover. SNIPPET only (insurancebusinessmag "Flood coverage gap puts pressure on Canada's broker channel"); UNVERIFIED.
F6. US benchmark: Verisk/ISO water-damage claim frequency ~2.05% of homes/yr (2013-17) vs 1.44% (2005-09); avg claim ~$10,000. SNIPPET (Insurance Business US); US, not Canada. UNVERIFIED.
Not found: Statistics Canada or CMHC claim-by-cause tables (searched; nothing usable surfaced).

## 2. Building age, plexes, basements, plumbing
F7. Montreal basement context: 80% of surveyed Canadians have a basement; 61% of those have a bathroom/sink/running water there; 1 in 10 had basement flooding; basement-repair cost +~20% 2019-2024. Montreal flooded 2017, 2019, Aug 2024.
 - Source: Insurance Business Canada, 27 Mar 2025 (Allstate/Leger poll + IBC), https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/ca/news/catastrophe/rising-water-damage-claims-push-home-insurance-costs-higher-report-530090.aspx. VERIFIED. Limit: poll, national, no claim-rate link.
F8. Pipe-life guidance: galvanized steel 20-50 yrs, copper 50+ (HUD guideline cited secondhand); pre-2006 PEX, polybutylene, cast iron and galvanized treated as high-risk by many carriers. Source: Insurance Journal "Building Updates: Aging Plumbing Systems" 2013, https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/features/2013/03/25/285518.htm and orion180.com. SNIPPET. Limit: guidance, no loss ratios; US.
F9. IBHS (cited secondhand): water-heater leak/failure risk rises steeply after 5 yrs; failed supply hoses averaged 8.7 yrs. SNIPPET. Limit: US, secondhand.
F10. Lead service lines: Montreal makes owners responsible for replacing lead entrance pipes (https://montreal.ca/articles/remplacer-son-entree-deau-en-plomb-votre-responsabilite-53006) - SNIPPET title only. Relevant to health/replacement, no claim data.
No quantified evidence found for: plex vs single-family, construction-period relativities, polybutylene/galvanized frequency in Canada.

## 3. Mitigation devices
F11. Smart auto shut-off (Moen Flo), LexisNexis study of 2,306 US homes: paid water claims -96% in year after install vs prior two years; severity -72%; comparable non-device homes +10% frequency. Moen whitepaper: joint customers -81% frequency, -85% severity (article notes selection bias/prior-loss customers may overstate).
 - Source: insurance-canada.ca, 28 Jan 2026 (sponsored by Moen), https://insurance-canada.ca/?p=84800. VERIFIED as reported. Limit: US, before/after design, vendor-funded; do not use as causal relativity.
F12. Canadian incentives: premium credits/rebates "about $75-$150" annually for verified installs (unnamed insurers). Same source; VERIFIED as stated; weak.
F13. Intact Centre (Feltmate, CBC 2024): insurers give 5-15% premium reduction for owners with flood-protection measures; ~80% of major Canadian communities subsidize backwater valves. Source: CBC, https://amp.cbc.ca/news/science/flooding-building-back-better-1.7346075. SNIPPET (via search summary); not fetched. Limit: premium discount, not observed claim reduction.
F14. Intact Centre "Water on the Rise" (2019): of 285 Ontario home assessments, 85% had no backup sump power, 53% never maintained backwater valve, 40% never maintained sump pump; 63% of 91 follow-ups acted. https://www.intactcentreclimateadaptation.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/Ontario_HFPP_Report_Executive_Summary_2019.04.18-compressed.pdf. SNIPPET; PDF unreadable -> UNVERIFIED. Limit: prevalence of risk factors, no loss outcome.
F15. Aviva Canada commercial leak-detection pilot (2-year monitoring); Desjardins says auto shut-off is the key element; HSB Canada sensor programs. No published results. SNIPPET (Insurance Business, Canadian Underwriter). No Co-operators data found.
Gap: no Canadian published claim-reduction % for backwater valve or sump pump (searched Intact Centre/ICLR, IBC). Mark "no quantified evidence".

## 4. Montreal specifics
F16. Aug 9-10 2024 (Debby remnants): "nearly $2.5B" insured (CatIQ initial), costliest insured event in Quebec history (above 1998 ice storm); published 13 Sep 2024. https://www.ibc.ca/news-insights/news/the-costliest-severe-weather-event-in-quebec-s-history-august-flooding-caused-nearly-2-5-billion-in-insured-damage. VERIFIED ($2.5B and date; claim count and damage-type not on page).
 - Later: ~87,100 claims, ~$2.81B incl. adjusting expenses (IBC "2024 summer of catastrophe" PDF, https://a.storyblok.com/f/339220/x/501c913069/2024-summer-of-catastrophe-across-canada.pdf) SNIPPET/UNVERIFIED; ~70,000 claims early count (Canadian Underwriter 21 Aug 2024) SNIPPET. Limit: Quebec-wide (~35 municipalities), not Montreal-only.
F17. Montreal by-law 11-010: check (backwater) valve mandatory on sewer line of NEW buildings since 2011; existing buildings only recommended. RenoPlex subsidy for houses/buildings <=5 dwellings, work >$500, property value <= $3,476/m2: examples $80 normally-closed valve, $560 per valve under existing slab, $1,500 sump pit + pump. https://montreal.ca/en/articles/sewer-backups-how-to-avoid-them-27249 (page dated 13 Nov 2026). VERIFIED. Limit: subsidy examples, not caps; uptake unknown. Implication: pre-2011 plexes/houses likely lack valves -> use as rating field (valve present, year installed).
F18. Aging pipes: 2013 city presentation average ages steel 84, cast iron 72, concrete 46 yrs; a Nov 2025 city presentation says 60% of local water network past 50% of service life; city: 83 Mm3/yr leaks (CBC: ~1/3 of water lost; Fraser Inst. 40%). Sources via search (CBC https://amp.cbc.ca/news/canada/montreal/montreal-water-infrastructure-problems-1.4660551; ceriu.qc.ca PDF). SNIPPET/UNVERIFIED. Limit: public mains, not private lateral/in-home plumbing; combined-sewer share not verified (no source read).
F19. Freeze-thaw / spring-thaw timing: no quantified claim-timing source found. UNVERIFIED gap (pipe-burst claims in Jan-Feb cold snaps, spring melt, and summer convective flooding are plausible but undocumented here).
F20. Montreal vs Toronto direct comparison: none found. Event-level only (F16, F21).

## 6. Deductibles and endorsements
F25. Sewer backup and overland flood are optional endorsements; standard policies exclude them (IBC https://www.ibc.ca/stay-protected/severe-weather-centre/flooding-and-insurance, VERIFIED). Desjardins Quebec: endorsement 16c ground water/sewer backup (Quebec only); 16d overland tied to the 16c limit and deductible (SNIPPET via PolicyMe). Third-party: ~40% to ~48% of homeowners lack these covers ("nearly half", F4).
F26. Deductible examples: a Quebec broker cited percentage-of-value deductibles (4%-10%) for water perils; unclear applicability. SNIPPET; UNVERIFIED. No evidence found on deductible-to-frequency elasticity in Canada.
