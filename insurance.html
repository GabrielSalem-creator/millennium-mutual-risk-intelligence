THE LONG-RUN MATH

Expected profit, not casino luck

Insurance profit depends on premium earned over coverage time, ultimate claims, expenses, and risk—not policy count alone.

Expected underwriting result
= earned premium − ultimate claims − claim costs − operating costs − reinsurance
How the law of large numbers fits

For independent, comparable exposures, aggregate claim averages can stabilize as exposure grows. Chebyshev gives a conservative bound: P(|mean − μ| ≥ ε) ≤ σ² / (nε²). Catastrophes, changing risk mix, and repeat claims break the simple independence assumption. Credibility weighting needs exposure and variance; this file is not enough to estimate it yet.
